Guidelines

RULES & GUIDELINES

Participants must be strictly between 10 and 25 years old on the event date (15 December 2026). Valid proof of age may be requested at venue check-in.
Participants must select the appropriate performance category during registration. Any changes to category must be approved by the organizer in advance.
Performance content must be appropriate for a general community audience. Profanity, hate speech, political defamation, or offensive content will result in disqualification.
Participants must report to KLE CBALC at the designated reporting time provided during slot confirmation. Late arrivals may forfeit their slot.
Exact time limits per performance category will be officially communicated by the organizers upon slot confirmation.
